# Editorial v2 Routing and Privacy Boundaries
Source: WEP-011 specification, owner review, current Phase5 PASS, and accepted Phase6 distillation.
Status: active project guidance; checkpoint 2026-10-05.

Use independent editorial_schema_version=2; keep shared ledger v1 unchanged.
Preparation is read-only and source-bound. The agent recommends optional
audience/format routes; owner choice is hash-bound before private drafts persist.
Blog, newsletter, social and video concepts are optional routes. A complete blog
may be paired with a concise newsletter summary and a distinct subscriber bonus;
a lesson may route to both brands when relevant, and no-route is valid.

Keep the original lesson private and identifiable for the owner's learning.
Create an anonymized derivative only after owner review, before any external use;
never silently replace or expose the private original. Fit/readiness never
overrides privacy. Validate held metadata and every supplied node. Unknown public
URL or capacity remains explicit. Variants share a slot but their effort adds.
Legacy reservations need an explicit unique predecessor to be replaced.

Review is source/route/decision/render-bound; material changes require fresh review.
An immutable manifest and final receipt define bundle completion; exact retries
are idempotent, and source/staging mismatch must not overwrite. Token coverage and
URL syntax do not establish prose quality or live-page verification.

WEP-011 Phase5/6/7 are complete for the bounded synthetic implementation. This
does not establish real-reader value, independent replay, or complete semantic
anonymization. WEP-010 remains owner-deferred; no runtime activation or publication
is authorized. Phase8, commit and push remain separate owner-triggered gates.

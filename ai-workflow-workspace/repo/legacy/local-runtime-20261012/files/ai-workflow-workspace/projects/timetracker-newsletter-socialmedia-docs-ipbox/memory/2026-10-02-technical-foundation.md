## Durable Constraints
1. One owning workspace per task/work entry. Stable identities and immutable
   revisions, not workbook names, titles or physical task vaults, drive joins.
   The committed manifest is the visibility boundary for multi-file transactions.
2. Actual time requires measurement or explicit human attestation. Use UTC elapsed
   time and local period boundaries. Unknown, estimate and confirmed zero differ.
   Weekly and monthly totals are alternative projections, never additive.
3. Preview is read-only; writes bind current source hashes and explicit approval.
   Recovery retains replaced bytes, rejects newer edits and resumes only its own
   journal. Locks are not OS-level compare-and-swap against non-cooperating editors.
4. Excel has protected derived tabs and append-only manual intake. Corrections use
   a new row and base revision; receipts, stable IDs and snapshots survive retries.
5. Recipient/purpose approvals bind current work hashes and safe text. Contractual
   routine care remains separate from recorded work. Share reviewed derivatives,
   never raw source material or private provenance.
6. Kit generation is not independent replay. Actual programmer evidence, signed
   kit-bound outcome and owner verification are distinct gates. Synthetic fixtures
   exercise those gates only; they cannot prove human independence.
7. Tax evidence completeness is not eligibility. Private financial/IP records
   retain taxpayer scope. Two workspaces may reference evidence without cloning
   time. Actual rules need applicable advisor approval and golden comparisons.
8. Personal PL and company EN drafts have optional translations sharing the same
   publishing capacity. Anonymization is not publication approval. No live sends,
   publication or model API calls are part of this implementation.

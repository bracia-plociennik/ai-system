## Residual Risk And Recovery

Archive QA verified 847 admitted files and preservation of 910 original files.
64 entries were excluded (including ten cache files and one dependency directory).
`git diff --cached --check` returned 2 for 72 inherited blank-line-at-EOF findings
only. These are disclosed non-blocking historical formatting findings, not a
clean diff claim. Reformatting would violate the byte-preserving archive intent.
New archive evidence passed its separate formatting check. Original assessment
content, results and owner approvals were not changed.

Private context, operational records, binary inputs, caches and dependencies are
excluded. The source checkout must not be deleted based on this partial archive.
Scanning and review reduce but cannot guarantee absence of every sensitive fact.
The branch inherits existing dev history; it is not a history sanitization.
Absolute paths and historical references may not resolve in a restored clone.
The archive is data-only; restoring or activating it needs separate approval.

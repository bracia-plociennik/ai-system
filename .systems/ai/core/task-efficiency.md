# Proportional Task Execution And Collaboration

Applies to answers, research, diagnosis, planning, implementation, integrations and
operations. Use within existing intake/routing, not as an extra mandatory phase.
The original owner outcome and actual source truth determine completion. Scope,
risk, required QA, approvals and source ownership remain independently binding.

## Minimum sufficient path

Identify the requested deliverable, observable completion, necessary evidence and
specific unknowns. A capability question may need an answer; a concrete request
for action needs the authorized action. Use conversation context to distinguish
these; clarify only consequential unresolved ambiguity. Do not turn a simple
answer into a project, a one-off change into a framework, or a handoff into consent.

Use known context and current evidence before asking. Group material questions;
resolve covered routine choices directly. Every additional read must address a
named unknown. Once evidence is sufficient, answer or execute. Continue research
only for an identified correctness/safety gap. Required startup/resume refresh
remains binding; expected same-scope edits do not justify repeated full discovery.

Prefer an existing proven mechanism. Build reusable automation only for demonstrated
repetition, complexity or safety need. Batch independent reads with bounded output;
keep edits and dependent checks sequential. Do not poll unchanged state needlessly.
Parallelism requires actual permission and useful independent work. Model choice
and task risk are separate; keep the current model and owner manual-only-agent
reservation. This contract never activates Luna-first or additional processes.

## Communication and progress

Lead with the useful answer/result. Match detail to the question, consequences and
uncertainty. Simple answers need no tool, plan file, timing log or ritual footer
unless current facts or governing requirements require one. Mandatory structured
intake/closure fields may live in the existing owned work record when the active
response contract permits it; do not omit required findings or owner decisions.

For ongoing work report discoveries, a concrete blocker and the next resolving
step. Do not repeat an unchanged status as progress. When progress stalls, inspect
scope growth, repeated discovery and irrelevant waiting; simplify or report the
exact blocker. There is no universal minutes budget or forced-write deadline.
Stop when acceptance is satisfied; extra tests/improvements require new evidence,
an actual dependency or an applicable mandatory gate.

Consolidate approval reference, scope, checks, result, limits and capture in the
existing task record. Workflow owns technical state; System holds coordination
pointers. Preserve history and private boundaries, with no per-answer artifacts,
automatic private-memory/profile promotion or new approval database.

## Bounded operations on data

A supporting operational organization route, not a micro-task, risk downgrade or
exception to critical human-led work. Eligibility needs an exact approved scope
and rule, explicit finite candidates, current application assumptions, safe access,
known triggers/side effects, concurrency protection, controlled restoration and
independent readback. Counts alone do not qualify an operation. A direct owner
instruction covering the exact action/rule/target need not be requested again;
a broad Auto flag, helper facts or copied handoff never supply that consent.

Keep candidate identifiers, old field values and reasons privately in the existing
record; do not copy full records/databases. Revalidate the record and classification
premises immediately before writing. Use a transaction and conditional write with
expected cardinality when supported; otherwise require a previously verified
mechanism with equivalent relevant safeguards, or stay on the broader local route.
Drift, changed rules, unexpected counts and transaction errors stop/rollback before
acceptance. Verify actual effects independently, including retained correct entries
and untouched fields. Unknown commit result requires read-only reconciliation,
never blind retry. Restoration must not overwrite a later concurrent change.

CMS/API filtering, relation requirements and empty-field normalization are actual
verification premises. An inactive item missing from an active-only API is not
proof of deletion. Check CMS/API state separately from published frontend state;
no build/deploy follows from a CMS edit. Keep project-specific operation notes
with that project's owner; no universal CMS tool is installed here.

Balances/billing, permissions, credentials, KYC, schema changes, history deletion,
blockchain and irreversible/unknown effects are excluded from this compact
organization route. Missing access or tool refusal permits a less sensitive
approved alternative, not bypass, broad shell enablement or forbidden export.
Prefer current version/checksum evidence to source export when sufficient.
Required review, plan/spec, production approval and evidence still apply; their
required content can be referenced/consolidated only where existing consumers
support it. Unsupported consumers retain their current gates.

## Supporting interface and evidence

Optional .systems/scripts/task_efficiency.py reads bounded non-sensitive JSON
facts from stdin and returns a recommendation with execution_authorized=false.
The closed task-efficiency-v1 facts contain kind, risk, explicit observed booleans
and operation safeguards only; no record values, commands, credentials or approval
store. It neither observes nor authenticates those booleans. The human/current
lead independently verifies actual approval, gates and evidence. Simple responses
do not require invoking this helper. Invalid/unknown facts reject; absent support
retains installed local gates. Existing evidence formats remain unchanged.

Use existing execution-efficiency timing intervals/union handling. Reasons may
identify read/analysis/write/verification/organizational work and tool/API waiting;
retain owner-waiting separately. Unknown observations are not zero, estimates are
not measurements, and overlapping stages must not inflate total elapsed time.
Measure time to first useful result separately from completion when observed.

Compare equivalent scenarios separately for answers, diagnosis, code, integrations
and operations. Preserve correctness, completeness, rework, necessary questions
and safeguards alongside action counts and time. Use at least three observed
samples per compared variant; label synthetic mechanism measurements separately
from end-to-end agent latency. Never manufacture a slow baseline or claim speed
improvement from shorter prose, fewer tests or synthetic success alone. No raw
conversation/client data is exported for benchmarking.

## Outcome, readiness and scope boundaries

For every task kind, distinguish the accepted deliverable from its verified quality,
deployment and actual enabled effect. In the existing intake/work record state the
observable completion, current bounded scope, exclusions and material prerequisites.
Check early whether data, access, a reference example, owner decision or environment
needed to verify or enable the result exists and is current. Use a short inline note
for small tasks, not another mandatory document, phase or schema. Do not require
code test terminology for research, writing, analysis or organizational work.

A missing activation prerequisite blocks activation, not independently authorized
work. Report which accepted outcome remains unavailable; deployed code is not an
enabled effect. Freeze a smaller deliverable before adding independent features or
a second release. Queue additions unless required for current correctness/safety.

Before reporting a blocker reconcile it with the latest relevant status and later
evidence. Historical failures are not current blockers. If current state is not
accessible, say unconfirmed and name the missing observation. Conversely an old
PASS does not prove current success. Do not rewrite old QA to resolve this distinction.

## Sufficient verification and justified repetition

Within the existing Check Plan each expensive activity names the question or DoD
it proves, why it is needed now and which changed inputs or actual boundary require
a repeat. This applies to reading, research, delegation, review, measurement and
testing. Repetition needs new relevant evidence, a changed hypothesis/input or an
applicable mandatory gate; elapsed time, habit and a desire for more evidence alone
are not reasons. A failed relevant check still requires correction and verification.

Fix command paths, metadata and fixture setup with focused checks before the costly
campaign. Verify affected behavior/dependencies after code changes; use artifact
consumers for artifact-only edits. Scale, benchmark variants and repeated fairness
runs need an actual performance/acceptance question. Research stops when the sources
suffice; an additional search must address a named correctness or safety gap.
Existing mandatory full CI, release and updater checks still execute fresh; unknown
dependency coverage never grants reuse. No universal smaller test count, arbitrary
time limit or risk downgrade is introduced. Stabilize/review the final source slice
before full-required checks to avoid preventable repeats.

Completion of the accepted goal permits "no blockers" or "nothing further required".
Separate optional improvements and requirements of a different goal (publication,
activation or formal project closure) from missing work for this goal. Do not create
a recommendation, alternative or extra task to fill a response quota. Actual
mandatory missing QA remains a blocker. Keep real owner final-approval gates.

## Objective process retrospective

On request review existing task artifacts/events; signal a clear anomaly briefly
from available evidence without automatically starting another model or full audit.
Distinguish inherent complexity, changed requirements, real product defects,
executor/setup mistakes, environment constraints, redundant work and insufficient
data. Each finding links evidence, proposed improvement and a future check. An
appropriate result is no significant inefficiency found. Do not invent a slow
baseline, model reasoning, minute attribution, percentages or savings. Compare the
whole path to accepted quality, including rework, coordination and owner effort.
Preserve raw/private sources with their owner; portable rules/examples are generic.
A retrospective recommends policy changes; it does not self-modify policy or history.

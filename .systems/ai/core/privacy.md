# Privacy Boundary

## Purpose

Protect customer data, private work materials, credentials, and local files from leaking into system-owned areas.

## System Areas

`.systems/` may contain only reusable rules, templates, examples, skills, scripts, and documentation.

## Forbidden In System Areas

- real client names or identifying facts;
- secrets and credentials;
- contracts and legal documents;
- production data;
- private emails, notes, exports, invoices, or customer files.

## Allowed

- synthetic examples;
- anonymized patterns;
- reusable process rules;
- generic placeholder names such as `example-client`.

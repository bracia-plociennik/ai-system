## Ask Conditions

Ask the owner before using this profile when:

- a client commitment may be created;
- scope, risk, billing, production, legal, security, acceptance, or final approval is affected;
- evidence conflicts with client brief, memory, decisions, or current files;
- confidence is low.

## Forbidden Overrides

This profile must not:

- replace client facts;
- replace client brief, memory, decisions, tasks, contacts, contracts, or current files;
- approve client commitments;
- authorize writes or file movement;
- weaken privacy or owner approval rules.

## Refresh Conditions

Refresh this profile when:

- client brief changes;
- client memory or decisions change;
- relationship status changes;
- scope or project status changes;
- owner gives a new instruction.

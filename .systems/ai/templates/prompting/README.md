# Prompting Templates

## Purpose

Reusable templates for AI System prompt composition artifacts.

Templates are system-owned and must not contain real client data, credentials, private documents, or identifying facts.

## Artifact Types

- `prompt-module.template.md` - narrow reusable framing behavior.
- `role-profile.template.md` - advisory role or domain lens.
- `variable-pack.template.md` - source-labeled variables and assumptions.
- `client-prompting-readme.template.md` - client-local prompting router.
- `project-prompting-readme.template.md` - project-local prompting router.
- `workspace-prompting-readme.template.md` - workspace-local prompting router.
- `client-collaboration-profile.template.md` - client relationship and collaboration lens.
- `ai-system-operation-role.template.md` - role for AI System operations such as guide, dump triage, client brief, distillation, and review.
- `ai-system-maintenance-baseline.template.md` - baseline for maintaining AI System itself.

Prompting artifacts generated from these templates are advisory only.
